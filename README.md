# Paper-Bird

Paper Bird is a side-scrolling arcade game where you guide a hand-drawn bird through gaps in stone pillars. 
Tap the Up Arrow (or your custom key) to flap and gain height — release and gravity pulls you down. Every pillar you clear adds a point to your score. Hit a stone or fly off-screen and it's over. Each run starts from zero, and if you beat your best score you'll get a congratulations message. 
The stone gaps are randomly placed every run, so no two games are the same. You can adjust the game speed on the fly with + and −, and tweak your flap key and screen resolution anytime in the Settings menu.
